# Water Warrior Privacy Policy

Water Warrior Privacy Policy
Effective date: August 2, 2026
Last updated: August 2, 2026
Water Warrior (“Water Warrior,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) is operated by Water Warrior.
This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, store, and protect information when you use the Water Warrior mobile application, website, and related services collectively referred to as the “Service.”
By using Water Warrior, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy.
1. Information We Collect
Account information
When you register for Water Warrior, we may collect:
Your email address
Your account identifier
Your username
Your display name
Your password or authentication credentials
Passwords are handled through our authentication provider and are not available to us in readable form.
Profile information
You may choose to provide information for your profile, including:
A profile picture
A display name
A username
A biography or other profile details
Your hydration goal
Other information you voluntarily add to your profile
Some profile information may be visible to other Water Warrior users,p’s features and your privacy settings.
Hydration and wellness information
Water Warrior collects information that you enter about your hydration activity, such as:
Amounts of water or other beverages consumed
Dates and times of hydration entries
Daily hydration goals
Hydration streaks
Progress and historical hydration records
Measurement-unit preferences
Custom beverage sizes or entries
We use this information to provide hydration tracking, progress calculations, reminders, statistics, and other app functionality.
Water Warrior is intended as a general wellness tool. It is not a medical device and does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment.
Friends and social connections
When you use the social features of Water Warrior, we may collect:
Friend requests
Accepted or declined friend relationships
User searches
Blocked users
Social activity associated with your account
Other users may be able to locate your account through information such as your username or display name.
User-generated content
When you use Water Warrior’s community or social features, we may collect content you submit, including:
Posts
Comments
Reactions or likes
Images
Captions
Reports of inappropriate content
Other communications submitted through the Service
Content you share may be visible to friends, selected users, or other members of the Water Warrior community, depending on the feature and applicable privacy settings.
Please avoid posting sensitive personal information that you do not want others to see.
Notifications
When you enable notifications, we may collect or process:
Notification preferences
Reminder schedules
A device notification token
Information required to deliver hydration reminders and social notifications
You can disable notifications through the app’s settings or your device settings.
Device and technical information
The Service may automatically receive limited technical information necessary to operate and secure the app, including:
Device type
Operating-system version
App version
Language and time-zone settings
IP address
Authentication and session information
Error logs and diagnostic information
Dates and times that the Service is accessed
[Remove references to diagnostics or error logs if your app does not collect them.]
Support communications
When you contact us, we may collect your:
Name
Email address
Message
Screenshots or attachments
Any other information you voluntarily provide
2. How We Use Information
We may use the information we collect to:
Create and manage user accounts
Authenticate users
Record and display hydration activity
Calculate progress, goals, and streaks
Provide personalized hydration reminders
Enable profiles, friends, posts, comments, and other social features
Send service-related notifications
Respond to questions and support requests
Investigate bugs, misuse, fraud, or security incidents
Enforce our policies
Maintain and improve the Service
Comply with applicable legal obligations
Protect the rights, safety, and security of users and the Service
We will not use health or hydration information for advertising or sell it to data brokers.
3. How Information Is Shared
With other users
Information may be shared with other Water Warrior users when necessary to provide social features.
Depending on the app’s functionality and your settings, other users may see information such as:
Your username
Your display name
Your profile image
Your hydration progress or streak
Your posts and comments
Your reactions
Your friendship status
Your email address and private authentication information are not intended to be displayed publicly.
Service providers
We use third-party service providers to operate Water Warrior.
Water Warrior currently uses Supabase for services that may include:
Account authentication
Database hosting
File storage
Backend infrastructure
Real-time app functionality
Supabase may process information on our behalf to provide these services. Supabase maintains its own privacy and data-processing documentation.
We may also use Apple services for app distribution, sign-in functionality, push notifications, purchases, or device-level permissions, depending on the features you use.
Service providers are permitted to process information only as needed to provide their services to us or as otherwise allowed by law.
Legal and safety reasons
We may disclose information when we reasonably believe disclosure is necessary to:
Comply with a law, regulation, legal process, or governmental request
Enforce our agreements and policies
Detect or prevent fraud, abuse, or security incidents
Protect the rights, property, or safety of Water Warrior, our users, or others
Respond to an emergency involving a risk of harm
Business transfers
If Water Warrior is involved in a merger, acquisition, financing, reorganization, sale of assets, or similar transaction, information may be transferred as part of that transaction. We will provide notice when required by applicable law.
4. Advertising, Tracking, and Sale of Information
Water Warrior does not currently:
Display third-party behavioral advertisements
Sell personal information
Share personal information with data brokers
Track users across apps or websites owned by other companies for advertising purposes
If these practices change, we will update this Privacy Policy and provide any notices or consent requests required by law or Apple’s policies.
5. Data Storage and Security
Water Warrior stores information using Supabase and other service providers that support the operation of the Service.
We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards designed to protect information against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, alteration, and disclosure. These safeguards may include authentication controls, encrypted network connections, access restrictions, and database security policies.
Supabase provides access-control tools, including database Row Level Security policies, that developers can use to limit which records users may access.
No storage or transmission method is completely secure. We cannot guarantee that information will never be accessed, disclosed, altered, or destroyed without authorization.
6. Data Retention
We retain account information and associated user data while your account remains active and for as long as reasonably necessary to provide Water Warrior.
When you request account deletion, we delete your account and associated personal information from our active systems promptly after the deletion request is successfully processed, except where limited retention is required by law, necessary to prevent fraud or abuse, needed to resolve disputes, or required to demonstrate legal compliance.
Limited copies of database information may remain temporarily in encrypted system backups for the duration of our service provider’s applicable backup-retention period. Backup copies are maintained for disaster recovery and are not used for ordinary business purposes. They are removed or overwritten according to the provider’s normal backup schedule.
Uploaded files that are deleted through our storage provider are permanently removed and are not recoverable through standard database backups.
Information that has been aggregated or de-identified so that it can no longer reasonably identify you may be retained.
7. Account and Data Deletion
You may initiate deletion of your Water Warrior account through the app by navigating to:
Account → Scroll Down → Delete Account
Deleting your account is intended to delete:
Your authentication account
Your profile
Your hydration records
Your friend relationships
Your posts and comments
Your uploaded profile images or other stored files
Other personal data associated with your account
Some information may be retained only where legally required or for the limited reasons described in the Data Retention section.
You may also request assistance by contacting us at wwcustomerprivacy@gmail.com.
Deleting the app from your device does not automatically delete your Water Warrior account or server-stored information.
Apple requires apps that support account creation to provide an in-app method for initiating deletion of the account and associated data.
8. Your Choices and Rights
Depending on your location, you may have the right to:
Access personal information we hold about you
Correct inaccurate information
Delete your account and associated information
Receive a copy of certain information
Object to or restrict certain processing
Withdraw consent where processing depends on consent
Appeal or submit a complaint regarding a privacy request
You may update certain information directly through the app.
To submit a privacy request, contact wwcustomerprivacy@gmail.com. We may need to verify your identity before completing the request.
You may also control:
Push notifications through the app or device settings
Photo-library or camera access through iOS settings
Other protected device permissions through iOS settings
Social visibility through any privacy settings provided in the app
Withdrawing permission may prevent some features from working.
9. HealthKit

Water Warrior does not use HealthKit
Water Warrior does not currently read information from or write information to Apple Health or HealthKit.
If HealthKit functionality is introduced in the future, we will update this Privacy Policy before collecting or processing HealthKit information.
10. Children’s Privacy
Water Warrior is not directed to children under 13 or the minimum age required to consent to data processing in their jurisdiction.
We do not knowingly collect personal information from children who are not legally permitted to use the Service. If you believe that a child has provided us with personal information in violation of applicable law, contact us at wwcustomerprivacy@gmail.com, and we will take appropriate steps to investigate and delete the information.
11. International Data Processing
Your information may be stored or processed in countries other than the country where you live, including countries where our service providers operate.
Where required, we take steps designed to provide appropriate safeguards for international transfers of personal information.
12. Third-Party Links
Water Warrior may include links to external websites or services. We do not control the privacy practices of third parties, and this Privacy Policy does not apply to information collected directly by those third parties.
We encourage you to review their privacy notices before providing information.
13. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes to Water Warrior, our information practices, legal requirements, or service providers.
When we make material changes, we may notify you email, or through another appropriate method. The updated policy will display a revised “Last updated” date.
Your continued use of the Service after an updated policy becomes effective means that the updated policy applies to your subsequent use of the Service, to the extent permitted by law.
