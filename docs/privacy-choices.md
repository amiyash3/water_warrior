# Water Warrior Privacy Choices

Water Warrior gives you choices regarding your account and personal information.

## View or update your information

You can update your profile and hydration preferences from within the Water Warrior app.

## Delete your account

You can initiate permanent account deletion in the Water Warrior app by navigating to:

**Account → Scroll Down → Delete Account**

Account deletion removes your Water Warrior account and associated information, subject to the limited retention circumstances described in our Privacy Policy.

## Notifications

You can change hydration reminder and social-notification settings within Water Warrior or through your iPhone settings.

## Contact us

For assistance with your information or account, contact:

*wwcustomerprivacy@gmail.com**
