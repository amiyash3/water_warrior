# Water Warrior Community Guidelines

Effective Date: August 2, 2026  
Last Updated: August 2, 2026

Water Warrior is a place to celebrate healthy hydration with friends. These Community Guidelines explain what is allowed so everyone can feel safe.

By using Water Warrior, you agree to follow these guidelines, our [Terms of Service](../terms-of-service/), and our [Privacy Policy](../privacy-policy/).

## Be respectful

Do not:

- Harass, bully, stalk, shame, or intimidate another person
- Use hate speech, including content that attacks people based on race, ethnicity, national origin, religion, caste, sexual orientation, sex, gender identity, disability, or similar characteristics
- Post sexual, pornographic, or exploitative content
- Threaten violence or encourage harm against anyone
- Promote self-harm, eating disorders, or other dangerous conduct

## Stay honest about health

Water Warrior is a general wellness app, not a medical service.

Do not:

- Share dangerous hydration or medical advice that could put someone at risk
- Encourage extreme or unsafe water intake
- Impersonate a healthcare professional or make false medical claims

If you have a medical concern, talk to a qualified healthcare professional.

## Protect people and privacy

Do not:

- Impersonate another person or misrepresent who you are
- Share someone else’s private information (such as address, phone number, school, workplace, or medical details) without permission
- Exploit, endanger, or sexualize minors
- Post illegal content

## Keep the community useful

Do not:

- Spam, scam, or send deceptive messages
- Run unauthorized advertising or phishing attempts
- Attempt to bypass moderation, reporting, blocking, or other safety systems
- Use automated tools to create accounts or manipulate activity

## Reporting and blocking

If you see content or behavior that violates these guidelines:

1. Use **Report** on a post, comment, or profile inside the app
2. Use **Block user** to hide profiles, posts, comments, and social activity between you and that person
3. Contact support at wwcustomerprivacy@gmail.com for urgent safety concerns

We may review, hide, or remove content, and we may restrict or terminate accounts that violate these guidelines.
